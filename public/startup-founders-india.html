What do startup founders in India need from their network?

Startup founders in India need four types of professional connections: (1) co-founders with complementary technical or business skills; (2) advisors and mentors who have built startups in the same sector; (3) angel investors actively looking for pre-seed and seed deals; (4) peer founders at similar stages to exchange learnings with. Build Your Network structures all four through its intent system.

Is there a network specifically for first-time founders in India?

Yes. Build Your Network is particularly valuable for first-time founders in India who don't yet have the IIT/IIM alumni network or the VC reference base. Which cities in India have the most startup founders?

Bengaluru has the highest density of tech startup founders in India, followed by Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and Pune.
