What is India's startup community?

India's startup ecosystem is one of the fastest-growing in the world — with over 100,000 DPIIT-recognized startups across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi, Pune, and hundreds of smaller cities. But most founders in this ecosystem network through fragmented WhatsApp groups, Twitter threads, and in-person events that exclude anyone outside the metro startup hubs.

Build Your Network brings India's startup community online — with intent-based profiles, GPS discovery, and structured connection requests that work as well for a solo founder in Coimbatore as for a VC-backed team in Bengaluru.

Every founder on BYN declares what they are building and what they need. This turns a fragmented WhatsApp community into a structured, searchable network where the right introduction happens in minutes rather than months.
