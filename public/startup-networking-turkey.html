Why Turkey founders connect with India's startup ecosystem

Istanbul is one of Europe and the Middle East's fastest-growing startup hubs — with a strong fintech scene (Iyzico, Papara), gaming ecosystem (Peak Games, Dream Games), and e-commerce innovation. Turkish founders building for global markets increasingly look to India's engineering talent pool for technical co-founders and product development teams.

The parallel is striking: both Turkey and India have large, young, digitally-native populations; both are building world-class products for their home markets and exporting that expertise globally. Indian engineers bring deep experience in payments, SaaS infrastructure, and consumer mobile products that directly complement Turkish commercial and product strengths.

Build Your Network's Worldwide mode gives Turkey founders direct access to this talent — filtered by intent, skills, and startup stage — without needing a conference introduction or warm referral.
