The core difference: intent vs history

LinkedIn shows you who someone was — their job history, education, past companies. Build Your Network shows you who someone is right now and what they are actively looking for. A first-time founder with a strong idea but a short resume is invisible on LinkedIn. On BYN, they are discoverable by co-founder intent, skill set, and location.

For startup founders, the question is rarely "what did this person do at their last company?" It is "are they looking for a technical co-founder right now in my city?" LinkedIn cannot answer that. BYN is designed to answer only that.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions from founders comparing the two platforms.

Is Build Your Network better than LinkedIn for startup founders?

For startup founder networking specifically — yes. LinkedIn is a general professional network optimized for job-seeking and content engagement. Build Your Network is built exclusively for founders, co-founder search, mentor discovery, and investor introductions. Every user declares their intent upfront, so there is no cold-messaging into the dark. For the startup use case, BYN delivers higher signal with less noise.

Can I find co-founders on LinkedIn?

Technically yes, but it is difficult. LinkedIn has no dedicated co-founder search filter. You must manually search, guess intent from a job title, and cold-message with low conversion rates. On Build Your Network, every founder explicitly sets their intent to "Looking for Co-founder", making co-founder discovery direct, filtered by city, and fully intentional.

How does BYN's matching compare to LinkedIn's algorithm?

LinkedIn's algorithm surfaces content from your existing network and paid advertisements. It is not designed for new relationship discovery — it is designed for engagement and upsell to Premium. BYN's matching is intent-first: founders who set matching intents (co-founder seeking, investor open, mentor available) are surfaced to others who need them, sorted by location proximity.

Which platform is better for investor outreach — LinkedIn or BYN?

BYN is more efficient for investor outreach if the investor is on the platform. On BYN, investors and angels explicitly set their intent to "Open to Angel Investing" or "Looking for Startups" — so founders can see which investors are actively looking, in which cities, and for which stage. On LinkedIn, investors receive hundreds of unsolicited pitches daily, so response rates for cold outreach are very low.

Is Build Your Network free compared to LinkedIn Premium?

Yes. Build Your Network's core features — profile, discovery, messaging, location filters — are free with 30 daily connections. LinkedIn Premium for job seekers costs ₹2,800–₹5,500/month in India. BYN Premium is ₹249/month or ₹599/quarter, focused on founder-specific needs like unlimited connections and priority discovery.

What does Build Your Network do that LinkedIn cannot?

BYN enables: (1) explicit intent-based filtering — see only founders actively seeking co-founders, not passive profiles; (2) GPS-based discovery within 10 km, 50 km, or 200 km; (3) profile score system that ensures users complete meaningful info before discovery; (4) no recruiter spam or job advertisements; (5) match expiry to keep connections active and intentional.
