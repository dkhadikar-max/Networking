How does investor-founder networking work on BYN?

Angel investors on Build Your Network set their intent to "Angel Investing" or "Looking for Deals". This makes them discoverable to founders who filter the discovery feed by investor intent — and makes founders with matching profiles appear in the investor's discovery feed.

Because both sides have declared their intent before any conversation begins, the first message already has context. An investor reaching out to a founder on BYN does not need to explain who they are or why they are interested — both profiles show the intent clearly. This is structurally different from LinkedIn, where investors wade through hundreds of cold pitch emails from founders they know nothing about.

BYN is free, runs in-browser, and available across India — including Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Pune, and all other startup hubs.
