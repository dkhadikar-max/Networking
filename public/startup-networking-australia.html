Why Australian founders connect with India's startup ecosystem

Australia has built a strong startup ecosystem — with Sydney's fintech hub (Afterpay, Airwallex, Zip), Melbourne's deep tech and health tech scene, and a growing export-SaaS community targeting US and Asian markets. The India connection is natural: Australia has one of the largest Indian diaspora communities globally, creating existing founder networks between both countries.

From a practical standpoint, India's time zone overlap with Australia is better than Europe or the US — Bengaluru is 4.5 hours behind Sydney (AEDT), making daily standups and real-time collaboration straightforward. Indian engineering talent also has deep experience in the fintech, SaaS, and consumer mobile verticals where Australian startups are most active.
