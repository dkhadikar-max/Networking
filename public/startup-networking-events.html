Why startup events alone aren't enough

India's startup ecosystem has a healthy calendar of events — T-Hub Demo Days, NASSCOM Product Conclave, TechSparks, IIT entrepreneurship fests, and hundreds of city-specific founder meetups. These events create valuable serendipitous connections. But they have a structural limitation: they happen once a month, attract the same local crowd, and depend entirely on whether the right person shows up on the right day.

The co-founder you need might be 8 km from you but miss every event you attend. The investor actively looking for your sector might not be in the city the week of the event. The mentor who could save you 6 months of trial and error might not attend Meetup events at all.

Build Your Network removes the event dependency. Your profile, intent, and skills are discoverable 24/7 across all of India. When someone with matching goals joins or searches, they find you — no event required.

Using events and BYN together

Startup events give you warm connections that digital platforms cannot replicate — a shared room, a live conversation, the energy of a pitch night. That serendipity is real and valuable. The problem is what happens after the event. Most connections made at startup events in India go cold within 48 hours because there is no structured follow-up infrastructure. Business cards get lost. WhatsApp groups get muted. LinkedIn connections get buried.

Build Your Network is designed to solve exactly this gap. When you meet someone at TechSparks, a T-Hub demo day, or a city founder meetup, the best next step is not to exchange numbers and hope you reconnect — it is to connect on BYN immediately. Their intent, skills, and current project are on their profile. You can send a direct message with context. The connection lives in a purpose-built professional network, not a social inbox competing with everything else.

The practical workflow: attend the event, have the in-person conversation, and within 24 hours find the people you met on BYN and send a connection request referencing where you met. Because both of you have declared your professional intent on your profiles, the follow-up message has immediate context — you already know what they are building and what they need. This converts warm event connections into lasting professional relationships at a rate that WhatsApp follow-ups cannot match.

Founders who use both consistently — attending relevant events for serendipitous discovery, then using BYN as the follow-up and ongoing discovery layer — build stronger networks faster than those who rely on either alone.
