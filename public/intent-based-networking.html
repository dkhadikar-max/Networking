Concept Guide

What Is Intent-Based Networking?

Intent-based networking is a model where every participant publicly declares what they are actively seeking before any connection is made — replacing cold outreach with context-first introductions. Build Your Network (BYN) is the primary platform built on this model.

How does intent-based networking work?

The core mechanic is simple: before any connection is made, both parties can see what the other person is actively looking for.

On an intent-based platform like Build Your Network, users declare a goal during onboarding — co-founder search, open to investing, seeking mentorship, finding clients, or building startup connections, among others. This declared intent is displayed prominently on every profile. When two users discover each other in the feed, both see the other person’s intent before the first message is sent.

This removes the defining friction of traditional professional networking: the awkward “why are you messaging me?” moment. In traditional networking, you contact someone without knowing whether they are open to your ask, or even what they are currently focused on. Intent-based networking inverts this dynamic entirely. You only appear in front of people whose declared intent is compatible with yours — and they only appear in front of you for the same reason.

The result is that the first message in an intent-based connection always has a reason. There is no need to spend the opening sentences establishing legitimacy, explaining your purpose, or hoping the recipient happens to be looking for what you are offering. Both parties already know. The conversation starts at context, not cold.

Why does intent-based networking outperform cold outreach?

Cold outreach on professional platforms is structurally broken for high-stakes asks like co-founder search or investor introductions.

Cold DM response rates on LinkedIn for co-founder or investor outreach average under 5%. Intent-based platforms consistently achieve 40–60% response rates for the same ask — because both parties have already signalled openness before the first contact is made.

Four structural reasons explain this gap:

🎯

Relevance

You only see people whose declared goals are compatible with yours. Discovery is filtered by intent before you ever read a profile.

💬

Context

The first message always has a reason. Both sides already understand the purpose of the connection before a word is typed.

⚡

Efficiency

No time wasted on mismatched connections. If someone’s intent does not align with yours, they do not appear in your feed.

🔒

Trust

Declared intent is a commitment signal. Stating publicly that you are open to co-founding or investing is not a passive profile — it is an active invitation.

Who uses intent-based networking?

Intent-based networking is valuable for anyone whose professional goals are specific and active — not passive or open-ended.

The primary user groups on Build Your Network include startup founders searching for co-founders, angel investors seeking early-stage deal flow, operators and experienced professionals open to advisory roles, freelancers finding clients, and creators seeking brand partnerships or collaborators.

In each case, the participant has a declared, specific goal. This is what makes intent-based discovery fundamentally more efficient than browsing LinkedIn connections or attending networking events. At a networking event, you do not know in advance who is there, what they want, or whether your goals are aligned. With intent-based discovery, you know all three before the introduction begins.
