Why Singapore and India's startup ecosystems are deeply connected

Singapore is Southeast Asia's leading startup hub — with a world-class financial infrastructure, EDB support for high-growth technology companies, and a founder community that has produced regional giants like Grab, Sea Group, and Shopee. India's startup ecosystem is the world's third-largest, with deep technical talent in Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Hyderabad.

The Singapore-India corridor is one of the most active in Asia: similar time zones (2.5 hours apart), English as the primary business language, strong Indian diaspora in Singapore, and an existing pattern of Singapore-headquartered companies using Indian engineering teams. Many successful Southeast Asian startups were co-founded by Indian and Singaporean founders who connected through professional networks.
