What to look for in a startup mentor

Not all advisors are the same. These are the qualities that matter.

🎯

Sector relevance

A mentor who built a B2B SaaS company is more valuable for your B2B SaaS startup than a general business advisor. Domain-specific experience saves months of trial and error.

⏱️

Recent operator experience

The startup ecosystem changes fast. A mentor who built a company in the last 5 years has more relevant context than one who exited in 2010. Look for recent builders, not retired executives.

🌐

Complementary network

The best mentors open doors you cannot open alone — introductions to investors, enterprise customers, or key hires. Look for someone whose network fills your biggest gap.

💬

Genuine availability

A mentor who has set their intent to "Open to Advising" on BYN has actively declared they want to help. This is a far stronger signal than someone who lists "advisor" on a LinkedIn headline they never update.
