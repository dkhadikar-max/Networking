Why Kenya and India's startup ecosystems are natural partners

Kenya and India share a remarkable parallel in digital financial innovation. M-Pesa pioneered mobile money in East Africa; UPI built the world's largest real-time payments infrastructure in India. Both ecosystems solved payments for populations that were underserved by traditional banking — using mobile-first, low-cost solutions that are now being studied globally.

For Nairobi-based founders building in fintech, agritech, healthtech, or logistics, India's engineering community has highly relevant experience. Indian engineers and fintech operators have built systems that serve hundreds of millions of users in conditions — infrastructure constraints, diverse language requirements, rural-urban gaps — that map directly to East African markets.
