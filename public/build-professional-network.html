The right way to build a professional network as a founder

Most founders make the mistake of trying to build a large network instead of the right network. A founder with 50 high-intent connections — a technical co-founder, two relevant mentors, and a few warm investor introductions — has more leverage than one with 5,000 LinkedIn connections and no real relationships.

The key is to lead with intent: know exactly what you need and make that visible when you reach out. Build Your Network is structured around this principle — every profile on the platform has a declared intent, so you see what each person is actively looking for before you send a single message.

Start with your immediate gap. If you are a non-technical founder, your first priority is finding a technical co-founder. If you have a working product, your priority shifts to advisor and investor introductions. Build Your Network lets you filter by these specific intent types — so every connection you make is towards a concrete goal.
