About BYN

What is Build Your Network?

Most founders in India spend 3–6 months trying to find a co-founder through LinkedIn cold outreach, startup events, and friends-of-friends — and most fail. The problem is structural: existing platforms weren't built for co-founder search. LinkedIn optimises for job-seekers. Events are one-off. Referrals are limited to your existing network. Build Your Network was built to solve this specific problem: connecting founders, operators, mentors, and investors based on declared intent — so both sides know why the connection matters before the first message is sent.

Build Your Network (BYN) is a free professional networking platform for startup founders and entrepreneurs in India — connecting users through intent-based matching where every profile declares what they are actively seeking.

How Build Your Network works

BYN uses intent-based matching. Every user states what they are actively seeking — a co-founder, mentor, investor, or collaborator. The discovery feed surfaces only people with matching intent and complementary skills.

🎯

Declare Your Intent

Every user sets a goal: co-founder, mentor, advisor, investor, or collaborator. This intent is visible on every profile before the first message.

🔍

Intent-Based Discovery

The discovery feed shows people whose intent and skills match yours. Filter by location (10–200 km radius), skills, and startup stage.

💬

Contextual Messaging

When a connection is accepted, both sides already know the purpose. Direct messaging starts with context — not cold introductions.

🔵

Intent Circles

Topic-based communities where founders share updates, ask questions, and collaborate around specific startup themes and industries.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Build Your Network?

Build Your Network (BYN) is a free professional networking platform for startup founders, entrepreneurs, and creators in India. It uses intent-based matching to connect users with co-founders, mentors, investors, and collaborators — based on what each person is actively seeking. BYN is available at buildyournetwork.online and works in any browser without an app install.

What does BYN stand for?

BYN stands for Build Your Network — the professional networking platform at buildyournetwork.online, designed for startup founders, entrepreneurs, and creators in India.

Who is Build Your Network for?

Build Your Network is for startup founders looking for co-founders or mentors, early-stage entrepreneurs seeking investors, engineers and product managers open to new ventures, creators looking for collaborators, freelancers seeking partners, and angel investors scouting early-stage deals. The platform is built specifically for the Indian startup ecosystem.

How is Build Your Network different from LinkedIn?

LinkedIn is a general professional network built for jobs and recruiter outreach. Build Your Network is purpose-built for founders seeking co-founders, mentors, and investors. The core difference is intent: every BYN user declares what they are actively looking for, so both sides of every connection already know the purpose before the first message. LinkedIn shows everyone; BYN shows the right people.

Is Build Your Network free?

Yes. Build Your Network is free to join. Free accounts include 30 daily connection requests, direct messaging, intent-based profile matching, and location-based discovery. Premium plans start at ₹249/month or ₹599/quarter in India, and $19/month or $39/quarter internationally. Premium unlocks unlimited daily connections, priority discovery, and advanced location filters.

Where is Build Your Network available?

Build Your Network is available across India — including Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Kochi, Gurgaon, Noida, and more. The platform supports local discovery (10 km, 50 km, 200 km radius) and a Remote/Worldwide mode for cross-city and international co-founder search.

What is intent-based networking?

Intent-based networking means every user declares what they are actively seeking: a co-founder, a technical partner, a mentor, an investor, a collaborator, or a peer. On Build Your Network, this intent is visible on every profile before the first message. It eliminates cold outreach and makes every connection immediately purposeful — both sides already know why they are connecting.

Does Build Your Network require an app install?

No. Build Your Network works directly in your mobile or desktop browser at buildyournetwork.online. No app install is required. An Android app is also available for users who prefer a native experience.
