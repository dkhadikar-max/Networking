## LONG DESCRIPTION (product pages, full about sections)

> Build Your Network (BYN) is a free, intent-based networking platform built specifically for startup founders worldwide.
>
> Unlike LinkedIn or generic networking apps, BYN matches you based on what you're actively trying to accomplish — finding a co-founder, raising a pre-seed round, exploring mentorship, or building partnerships. Every profile shows declared intent, so you skip the guesswork and connect with people who want exactly what you're offering.
>
> Key features:
> - Declare your intent: co-founder search, fundraising, mentorship, collaboration, or exploration
> - GPS-filtered discovery — see founders near you or search globally
> - Trust score and profile completeness scoring
> - Priority messaging for premium users
> - No cold email. No paywalls for core networking.
> - Available on web and Android
>
> BYN is live in 30+ cities across India, the US, UK, and Europe — including San Francisco, New York, London, Berlin, Amsterdam, Bangalore, and Mumbai.
>
> Free to join. No credit card required.

## GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE — SITEMAP SUBMISSION

**Time:** 2 min

1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click "Add property" → enter: `https://buildyournetwork.online`
4. Verify ownership:
   - Choose "HTML tag" method
   - Copy the meta tag content value (looks like: `google-site-verification=XXXX`)
   - Tell Claude Code the value — it will add it to the homepage `<head>`
5. After verification, click "Sitemaps" in the left nav
6. Enter: `sitemap.xml` and click Submit
7. You should see: Status = "Success" within a few minutes

**Sitemap URL:** `https://buildyournetwork.online/sitemap.xml`
